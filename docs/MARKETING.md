Keep original screenshots, blank device templates, prompts and the final composition separately under `output/marketing/` (git-ignored). Captures can contain third-party manga artwork and must not enter app resources or Git. Use real app screens, preserve their complete layout and aspect ratio, and keep the literal brand name `dokidoki"`.

Capture each device's own layout; do not stretch a smaller phone screenshot into a larger model. Current capture set uses iPhone Pro (1206 × 2622), iPhone Pro Max (1320 × 2868), and 11-inch iPad Pro (2420 × 1668 landscape). A smaller iPad requires its own capture. A generated hardware template is an illustration, not an official Apple marketing asset.

Install into existing preview simulators without uninstalling or resetting their data. Navigate through normal UI/public URL opening, wait for actual images, and capture with `simctl io screenshot` or `XCUIScreen.main.screenshot()`. For landscape XCTest captures, honor the image's orientation metadata; `XCUIApplication.screenshot()` can produce an incorrectly oriented app crop. Verify the decoded image visually. Temporary capture drivers must be removed; preserve the two permanent UI regression journeys and add no production screenshot hooks.

For clean status bars, use `simctl status_bar <device> override --time '9:41' --dataNetwork wifi --wifiMode active --wifiBars 3 --cellularMode active --cellularBars 4 --batteryState discharging --batteryLevel 100`, then clear the override after capturing. Record the app's actual bundle version and simulator models alongside the original PNGs. Full regression suites are unnecessary for artwork-only changes.
